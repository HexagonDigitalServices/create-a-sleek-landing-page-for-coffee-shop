Our Journey

Founded for the Thoughtful Coffee Drinker

Aura Coffee House started as a tiny 4-seat espresso pop-up in an old printing factory. Our belief was simple: city dwellers deserve coffee that respects both the land it grew on and the palates of those who drink it.

Today, we custom-roast all our micro-lots in small 5kg batches every Tuesday and Friday. We partner directly with 12 eco-certified farms, paying above market prices to ensure community sustainability and top-tier harvest selection.
